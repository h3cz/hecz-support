Hecz Support
============

Hecz Support is a remote support client made by Hecz (https://hecz.dev).
It is a modified build of RustDesk 1.5.0 (https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk).

What changed from upstream RustDesk 1.5.0
  * Name, icons, logo, and accent colors (Hecz branding).
  * Incoming sessions default to "accept by click": the person at the computer
    must approve every session. No password, account, or unattended access is
    preset. The computer's owner can change this in Settings.
  * About page links to hecz.dev and to the source code below.
